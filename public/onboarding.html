Retro Board Guide 📖

Everything you need to run your first retrospective in 10 minutes — from creating your account to exporting the minutes.

🚀 Quick start (5 steps)

  1. Create your account — click Login / Register, choose a username and password. Only the retro facilitator needs an account; participants join with just a name.
  2. Create a retro — from Home, pick a title, sprint (optional) and one of the 8 templates, or define your own columns.
  3. Share the invitation link — copy the link shown after creating the retro (it includes the invitation key) and send it to your team. They join with just their name, no login.
  4. Run the retro — everyone adds cards, votes with dots, and discusses in real time. Use the timer to timebox each column.
  5. Close the loop — turn the top-voted cards into commitments with an assignee and due date, then track them in the kanban.
🎨 Start your first retro

🔗 Inviting your team

Every retro is private. Access is granted only through the invitation link, which looks like /retro.html?id=7&key=abc123. Anyone with the link joins instantly with just a name.

  • Share the link in your team channel (Slack/Teams) at the start of the retro.
  • Participants appear in the Participants list; the retro admin can see who joined.
  • Enable anonymous mode when creating the retro if you want card authors hidden.
  • If someone loses the link, the admin can re-share it from the retro board.

🧭 Running the session (facilitator agenda)

  1. Set the stage (2 min) — remind the team of the sprint goal and the prime directive.
  2. Gather data (10 min) — everyone adds cards to the columns. Use the timer (e.g. 5 minutes) and enable anonymous mode if the topic is sensitive.
  3. Group and discuss (10 min) — use Auto-Group to cluster similar cards, then dot-vote on the topics that matter most.
  4. Decide (10 min) — take the top-voted cards and turn them into commitments: what, who, when.
  5. Close — check the Team Health Score, export the minutes and share them with the team.

✅ Following through on commitments

  • Commitments live in the kanban: drag them across Pending / In Progress / Done.
  • Each one has an assignee and a due date; overdue items turn red and due-soon items show an orange badge.
  • Track yours in the My Commitments page — the nav shows a red counter for overdue items.
  • Completing a commitment earns 10 points (+5 streak bonus if on time) on the global leaderboard.
  • Starting the next retro? Use the carry-over option to bring pending commitments into the new retro.

📤 Exports & integrations

  • Minutes (acta) — download any retro as a Markdown document with participants, cards, votes and commitments.
  • CSV — export the commitments of any retro for spreadsheets or Jira import.
  • JSON backup — download all your data from My Account.
  • Webhooks — send retro events to Slack, Teams or any webhook endpoint (configure and test it on the board).

🎬 5-minute demo script

Use this script to demo the tool to your squad in about 5 minutes.

  1. (0:00) Open Retro Board and log in. Show the Home page and the templates gallery.
  2. (0:30) Create a retro called "Sprint 42 Retro" with the Classic template.
  3. (1:30) Copy the invitation link and open it in a second browser/incognito window — join with another name.
  4. (2:00) Add a card in each column from both windows; show the real-time sync.
  5. (3:00) Vote on a couple of cards, then run Auto-Group to cluster similar ones.
  6. (3:00) Create a commitment from a top card, set an assignee and due date, and show the kanban.
  7. (4:00) Show the AI Summary, the Team Health Score and the leaderboard.
  8. (4:30) Download the minutes (Markdown) and show the final document.

🚀 Rolling it out to your studio

  • Start small: pick 2–3 squads to pilot it for one sprint.
  • Champions: nominate one person per squad to facilitate their first retro with the tool and gather feedback.
  • Share the guide: send this page and the templates gallery to your squad leads.
  • Collect feedback: agree on a channel (chat group, form, or retro about the retro) so improvements keep coming.
  • Iterate: new templates, columns and features can be added based on real usage — just ask.

❓ FAQ

Do participants need an account?
No. Only the person who creates the retro needs an account. Everyone else joins with the invitation link and their name.
Is my retro private?
Yes. Retro content is only visible to the retro admin and the people who join with the invitation link.
Is it free?
Yes — Retro Board is free for internal use.
