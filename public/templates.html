Free Retrospective Templates 📖

Pick a proven format, share the invitation link with your team, and turn feedback into commitments. No account needed to participate.

😄 Classic — Went Well / Didn't Go Well / Actions

The timeless three-column retro. Celebrate wins, surface problems, and leave with concrete action items. Best for teams new to retrospectives.

Best for: first retros, mixed-experience teams

🚀 Start / Stop / Continue

Forward-looking and action-oriented. What should the team start doing, stop doing, and keep doing? Great for process improvements.

Best for: process tweaks, quick mid-sprint check-ins

Use this template

😠😢😊 Mad / Sad / Glad

Emotion-first retro. Team members express how they felt about the sprint — great for surfacing morale issues that facts hide.

Best for: morale checks, team health conversations

Use this template

👍🧠🕳✨ 4Ls — Liked / Learned / Lacked / Longed For

A reflective format that captures what the team enjoyed, what they discovered, what was missing, and what they wish they had.

Best for: project wrap-ups, learning-heavy sprints

How it works

  1. Create a retro with your favorite template (free, no credit card).
  2. Share the invitation link — your team joins with just their name.
  3. Add cards, vote and discuss in real time on the shared board.
  4. Track commitments in the built-in kanban and earn points when you complete them.
